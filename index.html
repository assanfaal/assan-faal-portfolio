I'm Assan Faal, a freelance full stack developer based in Brikama, The Gambia, currently studying Information Systems at the University of The Gambia.

My focus is building the digital back-office that real, physical businesses run on — the booking system, the records, the receipts, the dashboard the owner checks every morning. My flagship build is a full clinic management system for a private dental practice, covering everything from patient records to revenue tracking.

Alongside that I take on WordPress builds for smaller clients, and I've volunteered as a graphic designer for PyCon Senegambia — so the design side of a project is never an afterthought.
